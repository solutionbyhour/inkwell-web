Features

Built for readers

Everything you expect from a modern comic reader, without the bloat.

📚

All Your Formats

CBR, CBZ, CB7, PDF, and EPUB — including native RAR5 extraction that other readers struggle with.

🔒

Completely Private

No internet connection. No accounts. No telemetry or analytics. Your reading list is yours alone.

📖

Manga & Webtoons

Right-to-left layout matching, auto-detected from ComicInfo.xml metadata. Continuous vertical scroll for webtoons.

✂️

Double-Page Splitter

Automatically splits wide landscape spreads into readable portrait screens, respecting reading direction (LTR/RTL).

📂

Flexible Library Roots

Choose any directory as your root. Subfolders display directly at the top level without redundant nesting.

🎨

Dynamic Cover Mosaics

Folder cover collages dynamically sample children cover art so you can easily browse subfolders.

🗂️

Shelves & Progress

Organize your collection with custom shelves. Automatically track in-progress, finished, and favorite books.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export your entire reading progress database as a single JSON file and restore it manually at any time.

✨

Onboarding Helper

A simple first-time guide that walks new readers through linking folders, layout settings, and shelves.

⭐

In-App Feedback

A quick shortcut in Settings to rate the app and leave feedback directly on the Google Play Store.

🛠️

Robust SAF Support

Seamless integration with Android's Storage Access Framework, external SD cards, and cloud storage providers.

🌙

Material Dark Theme

True black dark mode, orientation locking, and support for Material You dynamic colors on Android 12+.
